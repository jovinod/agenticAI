## Rubric

Treat as material:

- Fraud allegations or regulatory investigations naming the company.
- Recalls serious enough to affect revenue or safety.
- Executive departures tied to misconduct, not routine transition.
- Confirmed guidance cuts or covenant breaches.

Treat as not material:

- Analyst price-target changes without new underlying facts.
- Unconfirmed rumors without a named, checkable source.
- News already resolved or priced in over a prior reporting period.
- Ordinary product announcements or routine executive commentary.

When uncertain, prefer the less material classification and say why the
signal is ambiguous rather than forcing a verdict.
